Why This Works

OCPA's Smart Choice generation rate runs higher than SCE's standard bundled generation at every time-of-use bucket, and OCPA accounts also carry PCIA and MCAM exit fees on top of that generation charge. SCE's bundled generation is already PCIA-inclusive, so switching back to bundled removes both the Smart Choice premium and the exit-fee stack — same facility, same equipment, same delivery charges either way.

Battery Storage Assessment — TOU Arbitrage

Because your account is billed on energy only (no demand charge), a battery's value here comes entirely from time-of-use energy arbitrage: storing solar exported at a lower off-peak/shoulder credit and discharging it during the 4–9 PM on-peak window, instead of buying that energy back from the grid at the on-peak rate. We modeled this directly against your 15-minute interval data and the recommended SCE bundled rate schedule.

A ~30 kWh usable battery is the sweet spot — it captures most of the available arbitrage value ($1,193/yr) before returns flatten out. Your daily exportable shoulder-hour supply averages ~25 kWh, so capacity beyond ~30–40 kWh has little same-day solar left to charge from; a bigger battery mainly buys unused headroom, not more savings.

Methodology: intervals classified into SCE TOU-GS-1 Option E buckets (season + weekday/holiday + hour), current-day exportable energy capped at battery size and 90% round-trip efficiency, discharged against that day's on-peak import up to daily need. Value = avoided on-peak import cost minus the NEM export credit forgone at the off-peak rate. This is a production-data-driven estimate, not a substitute for a formal battery interconnection/sizing study.
